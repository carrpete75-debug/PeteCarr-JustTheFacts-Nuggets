Sources & notes

Dates, opponents and results come from ESPN. TV comes from the NBA’s official Nuggets schedule: national networks plus the Nuggets’ own broadcast (Altitude, KTVD, etc.). “TBD” means no TV is listed yet. After each game, the time is replaced by the result (W/L, score) and the running record. Preseason and regular-season records are counted separately.

Tickets appear for upcoming games only, and every link goes to an official seller. {{TIX_EVENT}} link to the specific game, taken from the host team’s official schedule (Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, the team’s own box office, or CU Athletics for Boulder). {{TIX_GENERAL}} go to the host team’s official tickets page because the team doesn’t publish a game link. No resale or affiliate links.
