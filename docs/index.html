Last game · Season opener · Thu Oct 22 · Thunder 96, Nuggets 84

Denver controlled the first half at Paycom Center, led by as many as nine and took a 48–44 lead into halftime. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander went 0-for-7 from the field before the break (AP). Oklahoma City outscored Denver 26–16 in the third. Ajay Mitchell’s layup gave the Thunder a 64–62 lead, their first since 3–0, and they led 70–64 after three. The Nuggets went 2-for-12 to close the third and scored four points in its last 6:30 (Denver Post). OKC pushed the lead to 93–76 with 1:44 left and won 96–84. There were only two lead changes. Per ESPN, Denver led for 62% of the game. Attendance was 18,203.

Both teams shot 38%: Denver 31-of-82 (8-of-30 from three, 14-of-22 at the line), Oklahoma City 36-of-95 (11-of-43, 13-of-14). The difference was ball security: Denver committed 22 turnovers (Murray 6, DeRozan 5), and the Thunder scored 21 points off them. Oklahoma City had 12 turnovers, and Denver scored 12 off those (NBA.com). Denver won the boards 53–46 (19 offensive), but OKC had 9 blocks to 2, 12 steals to 7, a 46–36 edge in the paint and 10–2 in fast-break points.

Aaron Gordon led Denver with 20 points (6-of-9, 3-of-4 from three, 5-of-6 FT) and 6 rebounds. Nikola Jokić had 13 points on 5-of-19 (1-of-7 from three), 7 rebounds and 5 assists in 35 minutes. Jamal Murray scored 6 on 2-of-11 with 10 rebounds and 3 assists; the Post’s Bennett Durando writes that Cason Wallace and Alex Caruso were “draping him.” DeMar DeRozan added 13 off the bench (5-of-9). Trevon Brazile had 12 rebounds in 18 minutes in his NBA debut, and Alpha Diallo (4 points, 3 assists in 22 minutes) also debuted. Two-way rookies Ryan Nembhard (5 points, 2-of-3) and Bryce Hopkins played in the final two minutes, along with DaRon Holmes II (3). Zeke Nnaji did not play (coach’s decision).

Injuries: Cam Johnson sprained his right ankle with about 3:30 left in the second quarter, after landing hard while contesting a shot by Mitchell (ClutchPoints). He was listed as questionable to return but didn’t come back; Diallo started the second half in his place (Denver Post; RotoWire). He finished with 5 points in 11 minutes. ESPN lists him Day-To-Day; RotoWire calls him questionable for Saturday “until further notice.” Before tip-off, the Nuggets held out Spencer Jones (hamstring). Adelman: “It’s not a serious thing, just precautionary. It’s one of those injuries that could turn out to be a serious thing … you can’t lose your backup power forward on night one” (Denver Post).

Thunder: Jalen Williams scored 21 (7-of-15), and Chet Holmgren had 12 points, 17 rebounds and 4 blocks. Ajay Mitchell had 12 points and 5 steals, Cason Wallace 9 with 4 steals, and Caruso 7 with 5 assists (+17). Gilgeous-Alexander finished with 17 on 6-of-19, ending his record streak of 141 straight 20-point games (AP). Oklahoma City is 2–0. Officials: Sean Corbin, Zach Zarba, Robert Hussey.

Betting result: DraftKings’ final ESPN pickcenter listing was OKC −7.5 (last evening’s card showed −8.5), total 236.5, DEN +270 / OKC −340. Oklahoma City covered by winning by 12. The 180 combined points went 56.5 under the total. Kalshi’s opener market settled OKC Yes.

Transactions: None for Denver overnight. NBA.com’s transaction log (checked ~5:05 AM MDT; latest league entries are still Wednesday’s) last lists Denver’s Oct 17 waivers of Dane Goodwin, Coleman Hawkins and Emanuel Miller. The Nuggets carry 17 players: 14 standard and 3 two-way (Hopkins, Whitmore, Nembhard), with one standard spot open. ESPN and nba.com list the same 17. Spotrac this morning still shows Denver at $217,460,512 active plus $666,667 dead ($235,008,821 total), $3,150,337 over the $221,686,000 second apron. Unchanged.

Holmes option: Hoops Rumors’ option tracker still lists DaRon Holmes II (fourth year; $5,547,137 there, $5,547,138 on Spotrac) with no decision. Spotrac still lists it as pending, with its deadline shown as Oct 31. Because Oct 31 is a Saturday, the deadline moves to Mon Nov 2. That’s also the night Denver hosts Portland.

Strawther: Out with a left knee sprain (re-evaluation six weeks from Oct 16; ESPN est. return Nov 29). He got no rookie-scale extension before the Oct 19 deadline, so he’s headed for restricted free agency in 2027 with a $7,723,090 qualifying offer (Spotrac).
