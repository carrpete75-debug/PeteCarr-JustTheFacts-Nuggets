Last game · Wed Nov 18 · Nuggets 105, Celtics 101 (at Denver)

Boston led after one quarter 30–24 and at halftime 61–49 (17-point first-half deficit). Denver won the third 31–19 (including a 20–4 run and a 4:35 Boston scoreless stretch) to take its first lead 69–67, then the teams traded leads repeatedly in the fourth. Murray hit a jumper with 6.4 seconds left for a 104–101 lead; Tatum was whistled for a technical after tossing the ball toward an official, and Murray made the free throw to seal it. DeRozan had 13 of his 18 in the fourth, including go-ahead free throws. Denver outscored Boston 56–40 in the second half.

Jokić: 24 points (9-of-17, 3-of-7 from three, 3-of-3 FT), 8 rebounds, 9 assists, 1 block, 2 turnovers, 4 fouls in 39 minutes. No triple-double (two rebounds and one assist short). Murray: 21 points (7-of-15, 2-of-6 from three, 5-of-5 FT), 7 rebounds, 2 assists, 4 steals, 2 blocks in 38 minutes. DeMar DeRozan 18 points (5-of-11, 7-of-8 FT), 13 in the fourth · Christian Braun 15 points (6-of-16), 5 rebounds, 3 assists · Aaron Gordon 13 points, 8 rebounds · Cameron Johnson 8 points. Denver shot ~43–45% from the field with limited threes; five players in double figures.

Celtics: Jayson Tatum 24 points (10-of-23, 3-of-9 from three), 8 rebounds, 3 assists, 6 turnovers (only 4 points in the second half) · Neemias Queta 17 points, 10 rebounds · Payton Pritchard 15 points off the bench · Derrick White 12 points, 8 assists · Paul George 10 points before fouling out. Boston shot 40.8% (40-of-98) and just 11-of-44 from three, with 18 offensive rebounds. Boston falls to 11–4.
