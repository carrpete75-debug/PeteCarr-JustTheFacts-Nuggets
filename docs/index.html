Regular season starts Thu Oct 22 at Oklahoma City. Preseason: 3–2 (final). Last season (2025–26): 54–28 (.659), No. 3 seed in the West, 2nd in the Northwest; lost West first round 2–4 to No. 6 Minnesota.

Last game · Preseason finale · Fri Oct 16 · Nuggets 121, Lakers 106

Denver closed its preseason with a 121–106 win at Crypto.com Arena, finishing 3–2. The Nuggets scored 36 in the first quarter for a 36–22 lead and led by as many as 17. The Lakers outscored Denver 33–26 in the second, so it was 62–55 at halftime. Denver led 88–83 after three, then won the fourth 33–23. Per ESPN, the Nuggets led for 96% of the game. Denver shot 46-of-79 (58%), 11-of-27 (41%) from three and 18-of-25 at the line. Los Angeles went 35-of-82 (43%), 17-of-43 (40%) and 19-of-25. Denver had a 56–30 edge in paint points and 12 steals to the Lakers’ 5, plus 32 assists to 26 and 45 rebounds to 39. Both teams had 14 turnovers. Attendance was 19,057.

With Aaron Gordon held out for rest, Trevon Brazile started alongside Murray, Braun, Cam Johnson and Jokić (Law Murray / The Athletic via RotoWire). Brazile had 12 points on 5-of-7, 8 rebounds, 2 steals and 2 blocks in 27 minutes. Jamal Murray led all scorers with 21 (8-of-15, 5-of-5 FT) and 6 assists. Nikola Jokić had 17 points on 7-of-8 (3-of-4 from three), 6 rebounds, 8 assists and 6 turnovers in 31 minutes. Christian Braun scored 16 and finished +18. Cam Johnson had 12 on 5-of-8 before he was ejected in the third quarter: a review upgraded his elbow to Austin Reaves’ face to a Flagrant 2 (USA Today; Lakers Nation). NBC Sports/Rotoworld notes the foul carries no regular-season punishment. Off the bench, DeMar DeRozan scored 14 (6-of-9) with 5 assists in 27 minutes, Marvin Bagley III had 13 points and 8 rebounds, and Tyus Jones had 8 assists without scoring. The Denver Gazette reports a nine-man rotation through three quarters.

For the Lakers, Austin Reaves scored 19 (8-of-12 FT). Luka Dončić had 15 in 19 first-half minutes, and Ziaire Williams had 14 on 5-of-6 in 11 minutes; both sat after halftime (Lakers Nation). Sandro Mamukelashvili had 15 and 8 rebounds, Jaden Hardy 13 and Quentin Grimes 11. DraftKings’ final ESPN pickcenter listing was LAL −3.5, total 231.5, DEN +124 (the evening card had LAL −5.5 / 229.5 / DEN +185). Denver won outright by 15, and the 227 points went under. Kalshi’s Oct 16 DEN market settled Yes.

Strawther diagnosis: Before the finale, the Nuggets announced that Julian Strawther has a left knee sprain from Wednesday’s game at the Clippers and will be re-evaluated in six weeks (AP/ESPN; Denver Gazette; Hoops Rumors). Adelman, before the game: “We were gonna rely on Jules a lot, especially on the offensive end … It’s unfortunate … especially with the timing for him” (DenverSports.com). His rookie-scale extension deadline passed Monday at 4:00 PM MT (5:00 PM CT) without a deal, so he is headed for restricted free agency in 2027 (Hoops Rumors).

Transactions: None for Denver today. NBA.com’s transaction log (latest league entries Oct 19) and Hoops Rumors’ Nuggets feed still end with Saturday’s moves: Denver waived G Dane Goodwin, F Coleman Hawkins and F Emanuel Miller, its three Exhibit 10 players. All three cleared waivers Monday with no claim, and Denver owes no dead money for them. The Nuggets carry 17 players: 14 standard and 3 two-way (Hopkins, Whitmore, Nembhard), with one standard spot open. ESPN and nba.com list the same 17. Spotrac lists today, Oct 20 (opening night), as the date the three two-way deals become 50% guaranteed. Spotrac’s team cap tracker this afternoon still shows Denver at $217,460,512 active plus $666,667 dead, unchanged.

Holmes option: Several teams exercised 2027–28 rookie-scale options today: Chicago (Buzelis, Essengue), Cleveland (Tyson), Detroit (Holland), Toronto (Murray-Boyles, Walter) and Memphis (Edey, Coward), per Hoops Rumors. Its option tracker lists DaRon Holmes II (fourth year, $5,547,137 there; Spotrac $5,547,138) with no decision yet. The deadline is Mon Nov 2 because Oct 31 falls on a Saturday.

Extension outcome: The deadline passed Monday at 4:00 PM MT with no Strawther extension. Hoops Rumors’ Monday-night recap counts seven rookie-scale extensions league-wide, the fewest since 2018, and lists Strawther among the 14 2023 first-rounders who are now headed for contract years and possible restricted free agency in 2027. NBA.com’s transaction log shows no Denver extension. DenverSports.com’s Jake Shapiro (Monday, ~8:02 PM MDT) notes Denver keeps control through restricted free agency: it can match any offer sheet as long as it makes the qualifying offer. Strawther, the 29th pick in 2023, plays out the fourth year of his rookie deal ($4,826,931). His qualifying offer is $7,723,090 (Spotrac; Over the Apron agrees). Before the deadline, DNVR’s Brendan Vogt wrote that the Nuggets “have grown wary of generous and hopeful extensions.” Around the division, Minnesota agreed to a one-year extension with Jaden McDaniels on Monday evening (Hoops Rumors).

Rotation: Adelman said after Monday’s practice at Ball Arena that the top six is clear, with DeMar DeRozan joining the starters. Tyus Jones, Marvin Bagley III and a healthy Spencer Jones make nine, which leaves one or two spots for the opener (Denver Gazette, Vinny Benedetto, Monday ~7:30 PM MDT). Alpha Diallo, 29, the reigning EuroLeague best defender on a one-year deal, is in that mix for his NBA debut Thursday. On Trevon Brazile: “We haven’t decided that yet. We have the week to go … I think the first two games, we’ll keep the (whole team) together,” Adelman said; Brazile, Cam Whitmore and Bryce Hopkins are candidates for what he calls a “fake 11-man rotation” (Denver Post, Bennett Durando, today ~4:25 PM MDT). Adelman’s regular-season goal: “Our expectation is trying to get in the top six” in the West (Denver Post, Monday). He also joined 92.5 Altitude Sports Radio this afternoon ahead of the opener.
