Last game · Preseason · Wed Oct 14 · Clippers 122, Nuggets 110

Denver lost 122–110 at Intuit Dome in its fourth preseason game (now 2–2). The score was tied 24–24 after one quarter. Los Angeles led 58–55 at halftime, then outscored Denver 38–26 in the third and closed 26–29 in the fourth. Attendance was 13,018. Denver shot 41-of-86 (48%) from the field, 11-of-33 (33%) from three and 17-of-30 (57%) at the line; the Clippers went 45-of-87, 15-of-33 and 17-of-20. Denver had 44 rebounds to L.A.’s 40, 29 assists to 35, and 16 turnovers to 11. Both teams scored 18 fast-break points; Denver had 54 points in the paint to L.A.’s 50.

The usual starters opened and played short first-half-heavy stints: Jamal Murray (17 min, 14 points on 6-of-8), Christian Braun (13 min, 3 points), Cam Johnson (13 min, 5), Aaron Gordon (14 min, 8 points, 6 rebounds) and Nikola Jokić (17 min, 5 points, 6 rebounds, 7 assists). Off the bench, Trevon Brazile led Denver with 22 points (8-of-10, 3-of-4 from three) in 25 minutes, and Cam Whitmore scored 17 (7-of-13) in 24. Julian Strawther had 11 points (3-of-7 from three) in 19 minutes before exiting in the third quarter with a left-knee injury; he was scheduled for an MRI Thursday morning (Katy Winge / Altitude Sports; Adelman: “All you can do is hope for the best”). Results of that MRI were still not published as of this evening edition. DaRon Holmes II scored 8 in 12 minutes. DeMar DeRozan (rest) and Spencer Jones (hamstring) did not play.

For the Clippers, Rui Hachimura scored 16, Baba Miller 15 off the bench, Derrick Jones Jr. and Darius Garland 14 each, and Keaton Wagler 12. DraftKings’ final ESPN pickcenter listing was Denver −5.5, total 226.5, ML −218 (evening card had been −4.5 / 225.5 / −170); Denver lost by 12 and the 232 points went over.

Transaction note (unchanged since morning): The Nuggets signed and waived center Steven Crowl on Oct 14 (NBA.com transaction log / Hoops Rumors / AP). ESPN’s roster API still lists Crowl and waived Lonnie Walker IV (cleared waivers Oct 14); nba.com lists 20 and matches the contracts file. Active roster count remains 20 (14 standard, 3 two-way, 3 Exhibit 10) with waived footnotes for the ESPN lag. No new Denver transaction this afternoon.
