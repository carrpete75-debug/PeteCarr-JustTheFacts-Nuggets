How last season ended · West 1st Round vs Timberwolves

Denver won Game 1 at Ball Arena 116–105, then dropped Game 2 at home 119–114 and both games in Minneapolis (113–96, 112–96). A 125–113 home win in Game 5 forced a Game 6, which Minnesota won 110–98 on Thursday, Apr 30 at Target Center.

In the clincher the Timberwolves, missing their top three guards to injury, went big and won the paint 64–40 and the glass 50–33 (AP). Jaden McDaniels scored 32 with 10 rebounds; Terrence Shannon Jr. added 24 in a surprise start; Rudy Gobert had 10 points, 13 rebounds and 8 assists. For Denver, Nikola Jokić had 28 points, 9 rebounds and 10 assists and Cam Johnson scored 27 (5-of-10 from three). Jamal Murray shot 4-of-17 for 12.

Carry-forward: At Monday’s media day, head coach David Adelman called the playoffs “a bad 10 days” and said he put too much on Murray last season. His stated camp focus is transition defense. Peyton Watson was traded to Cleveland; DeMar DeRozan signed a one-year deal; Dave Joerger joined as top assistant.

Ball Arena & team news

nuggets.com news

Camp opened Tue Sep 29 in Boulder after Monday’s media day at Ball Arena. Arena: KSE’s ~$135M Ball Arena renovation was announced Sep 18. No new arena item since then.

  • Jokić leaves no doubt on Nuggets future, says he will re-sign ESPN · Sep 28, 2026

    Jokić said he plans to sign his extension when it’s offered next summer.

  • Media day: front office on Peyton Watson trade, DeMar DeRozan on role Denver Post (via Kano) · Sep 28, 2026

    Adelman worries about leaning too hard on Murray against full-court pressure. Tyus Jones is slated as the backup point guard, and Cam Johnson is expected to handle the ball more.

  • Aaron Gordon adjusts routine; Cam Whitmore shines on day one Denver Gazette · Sep 29, 2026

    Gordon and Whitmore were the first names out of Adelman’s mouth after the first practice. Dave Joerger joins as top assistant to “help with the offense.”

  • KSE announces $135 million Ball Arena renovations Denver Gazette · Sep 18, 2026

    The plan adds a new glass-front east entrance facing downtown, wider concourses and three new premium clubs. It’s privately funded and phased: construction starts summer 2027, with completion targeted for the 2028–29 seasons. A Speer Blvd pedestrian bridge is expected around 2029.

  • Ball Arena to undergo massive renovation (KSE release) ColoradoAvalanche.com · Sep 18, 2026

    Official release. A Site Development Plan was submitted to the City of Denver, and River Mile redevelopment is to follow.
