Last game · Preseason · Tue Oct 6 · Nuggets 117, Jazz 106

Denver beat Utah 117–106 at the Delta Center, two days after losing 109–97 to the Jazz in Boulder. Utah led 31–24 after one quarter. The Nuggets then scored 41 in the second quarter to lead 65–59 at halftime and were never behind again. Denver’s biggest lead was 21 and Utah’s was 9. The Nuggets shot 53% from the field and 11-of-28 (39%) from three, and they went 30-of-42 at the line; Utah went 12-of-22. Utah had more rebounds (43–41) and offensive rebounds (15–7). Denver had 16 fast-break points to Utah’s 0.

Jamal Murray returned after missing the opener for personal reasons. He started with Christian Braun, Cam Johnson, Aaron Gordon and Nikola Jokić. No starter played more than 16 minutes, and the starters and DeMar DeRozan sat the second half (Denver Gazette). Gordon led Denver with 18 points (5-of-7, 6-of-7 FT) in 15 minutes. Jokić had 6 points, 4 rebounds and 5 assists in 16 minutes and drew a technical foul after a shoving exchange with Jaxson Hayes (Eurohoops; Denver Gazette). Murray had 4 points (1-of-4) and 3 assists. Off the bench, Julian Strawther scored 15 (3-of-4 from three) and Cam Whitmore scored 14 in 15 minutes. Marvin Bagley III had 10 points and was a team-best +15, and Alpha Diallo went 3-of-3 (2-of-2 from three) for 8 points. Rookie Darryn Peterson led Utah with 23. The Jazz played without Lauri Markkanen (neck soreness) and Jusuf Nurkić (foot).

The Denver Post’s Bennett Durando said the bench has three clear sources of offense: DeRozan’s midrange game, Strawther’s shot creation and the Tyus Jones–Bagley pick-and-roll. With Spencer Jones out, Adelman’s all-bench unit used DeRozan at power forward. Durando wrote that if DeRozan, Spencer Jones and Bagley are locks, then Tyus Jones, Strawther and Diallo are competing for the last two spots in a 10-man rotation.

Overnight: No Nuggets roster transaction. RealGM’s league log for Oct 6–7 lists moves by Orlando, Oklahoma City, Utah and the Clippers, plus four players clearing waivers, none involving Denver. Denver’s latest move is still Sep 30 (Ryan Nembhard signed to a two-way, KJ Simpson waived). ESPN and nba.com both list 21 players. Next up is Denver’s only preseason home game: Sunday vs. Chicago at Ball Arena.
