Last game · Wed Oct 28 · Nuggets 104, Pelicans 91

Denver never trailed (AP). Murray made his first three shots, all threes, and Denver shot 62% in the first quarter to lead 37–18. Bagley’s runner made it 44–22 with about nine minutes left in the second, Denver’s largest lead (AP). New Orleans won the second and third quarters and cut it to 88–83 on a Zion Williamson three in the fourth. Murray answered with a three and a driving finger roll to push it back to 10 (AP). Nuggets.com credited Braun’s late threes with sealing it. Denver won the fourth 25–18.

Jokić: 21 points (8-of-13, 5-of-5 FT), 13 rebounds, 10 assists, a steal and 7 turnovers in 34 minutes, plus-17. That’s his 200th regular-season triple-double. He and Westbrook are the only players past 200, and per ESPN Research, Westbrook reached 200 in his 1,175th game; Jokić did it in his 814th. Sixteen of Jokić’s triple-doubles have come against New Orleans, six more than against any other team (ESPN). Murray 23 (9-of-19, 5-of-11 from three) and 6 assists. Gordon 15 and 11 rebounds, his first double-double of the season (Nuggets.com), in 31 minutes; he played, two nights after his ejection. Braun 15 (3-of-8 from three) and 3 steals. Brazile started at forward with Cam Johnson out: 11 points and 8 rebounds (Hoops Rumors via the Gazette). Off the bench: Bagley 8, DeRozan 6 and 5 assists, T. Jones 5, Hopkins 0 in 14 minutes. Denver used nine players; Nnaji, Holmes II, Whitmore and Nembhard didn’t play (coach’s decision).

Team: Denver shot 46% (37-of-80), 12-of-31 from three and 18-of-19 at the line, with 30 assists and 11 steals. New Orleans made 7 of 30 threes (23.3%). Pelicans: Williamson 25 on 10-of-14; Queen 11 and 12; Fears 11; D. Murray 10 and 8. New Orleans is 0–4. Attendance: 16,334.
