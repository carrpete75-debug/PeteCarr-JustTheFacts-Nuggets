The Nuggets briefing

Nuggets 141, Pelicans 135 (OT). Denver trailed by 23 in its home opener, was down 108–89 after three, and won in overtime. Jamal Murray scored 42 (8-of-16 from three). Nikola Jokić had 35 points, 15 rebounds and 10 assists, his 199th regular-season triple-double: 10 to tie Russell Westbrook’s record of 209, 11 to break it. Rookie Trevon Brazile added 17 and 9. Last game · Home opener · Sat Oct 24 · Nuggets 141, Pelicans 135 (OT)

New Orleans scored 38 points in the first quarter on 15-of-21 shooting (DenverSports), led 53–30 in the second quarter and 73–57 at halftime after shooting 65.9% in the first half (AP). The Ball Arena crowd booed. The Pelicans’ lead reached 23 twice, the second time at 95–72 midway through the third, and they led 108–89 entering the fourth. Jokić and Murray combined for 22 of Denver’s 32 third-quarter points (AP). A bench-heavy group of Tyus Jones, DeRozan, Brazile, Gordon and Bagley III cut it to 10 with a little more than eight minutes left (Denver Gazette). Then Jokić and Murray hit threes, Gordon had a reverse dunk and later an alley-oop from Jokić, and Brazile picked off a Williamson pass. Murray was fouled on a three with 16.8 seconds left and made two of three to tie it at 126. Jokić’s pressure forced Trey Murphy III to lose the ball out of bounds with 1 second left (DenverSports).

Overtime: Denver outscored New Orleans 15–9. Jokić had 8 points and 7 rebounds in the extra period. Brazile blocked Saddiq Bey’s three on the first possession, hit a corner three for a 133–129 lead with 1:25 left, and scored the final putback. The Pelicans went 1-of-11 in overtime (DenverSports; AP). Denver held New Orleans to 18 in the fourth quarter (Gazette). Per DenverSports, coming back from 19 down entering the fourth ties a franchise record (2019 vs Philadelphia), and the 23-point comeback ties the largest at home in club history (2020 vs San Antonio). Nuggets.com calls it the sixth 22-point second-half comeback in franchise history. Attendance: 19,905.

Murray: 42 points on 12-of-25, 8-of-16 from three and 10-of-13 at the line, with 6 assists, two days after a 6-point night at OKC. DenverSports says the eight threes are the most by a Nugget in a home opener. Jokić: 35 points (12-of-23, 3-of-10 from three, 8-of-8 FT), 15 rebounds, 10 assists, 2 steals, 3 blocks and 6 turnovers in 44 minutes. Brazile, in his second NBA game: 17 points (6-of-11, 3-of-7), 9 rebounds, 2 blocks, both on three-point attempts; he played all of the fourth and overtime. Braun 15 and 8 rebounds, DeRozan 12, Gordon 7 (2-of-9) with 5 rebounds and 4 assists. Two-way rookie Bryce Hopkins started (2 points in 19 minutes). Nnaji, Holmes II and Nembhard did not play (coach’s decision); Cam Johnson, Diallo, Spencer Jones and Strawther were out.

Jokić after the game, on fans who booed: “We got booed at our first home game, and I think those fans who booed us, they don’t need to come anymore … So, if they ask me, they are not welcome anymore” (ESPN). Adelman: “We were dismal the first half. It felt like we were waiting for our offense to wake ourselves up, and in the meantime, they were laying the ball in” (DenverSports; Gazette).

Triple-double milestone: The Gazette and DenverSports report that this was Jokić’s 222nd triple-double counting regular season and playoffs combined, passing Westbrook for the most in NBA history by that measure. The official regular-season record, which the tracker follows, is still Westbrook’s 209; Jokić has 199 (Basketball-Reference, checked this morning; AP and NBA.com agree).

Pelicans: Murphy III 26, Dejounte Murray 22, Derik Queen 17, Zion Williamson 17, Bey 14; ex-Nugget DeAndre Jordan 9 and 9 in 20 minutes. New Orleans had a 70–56 edge in the paint and 23–12 in fast-break points, but committed 18 turnovers to Denver’s 12. The Pelicans are 0–3. Officials: Scott Foster, John Butler, Nate Cearley.

Betting result: DraftKings closed DEN −11.5, total 239.5, ML DEN −485 (ESPN pickcenter). Denver won by 6, so New Orleans covered. The 276 combined points went 36.5 over. Kalshi’s market settled DEN Yes.

Transactions: None for Denver overnight. Spotrac’s Nuggets log and Hoops Rumors’ Nuggets feed (checked ~5:20 AM MDT) still last list the Oct 17 waivers. Roster: 17, 14 standard and 3 two-way (Hopkins, Whitmore, Nembhard), one standard spot open; ESPN and nba.com list the same 17. Spotrac: $217,460,512 active plus $666,667 dead, $3,150,337 over the $221,686,000 second apron. Unchanged. Report only, not a transaction: Hoops Rumors, citing Jake Fischer (The Stein Line), says Milwaukee turned down a pre-cutdown offer of Zeke Nnaji, two future seconds and a second-round swap for Kevin Porter Jr., because it didn’t want Nnaji’s $7.47M 2027–28 player option.

Holmes option: No decision reported on DaRon Holmes II’s fourth-year option ($5,547,138 on Spotrac, which lists its deadline as Oct 31; since that’s a Saturday, it moves to Mon Nov 2). He did not play Saturday.
