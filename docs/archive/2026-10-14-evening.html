Last game · Preseason · Sun Oct 11 · Nuggets 133, Bulls 128

Denver beat Chicago 133–128 at Ball Arena in its only preseason home game. Denver led 29–27 after one quarter. Chicago scored 41 in the second quarter, led by as many as 15 (55–40 with 5:56 left in the half) and took a 68–59 halftime lead. Denver won the third quarter 40–26 and went ahead for good at 90–89 with 1:28 left in the period. It led 99–94 after three. The lead changed hands 11 times; Denver’s biggest lead was 8. Both teams shot 51% from the field. Chicago made 21 of 50 threes to Denver’s 13 of 32. Denver went 32-of-46 at the line; Chicago went 13-of-18. Denver outrebounded Chicago 48–45 (16–13 on the offensive glass) and outscored it 52–48 in the paint. Chicago had 16 fast-break points to Denver’s 10.

The usual starters went again: Jamal Murray, Christian Braun, Cam Johnson, Aaron Gordon and Nikola Jokić. Gordon led Denver with 25 points (9-of-12, 3-of-4 from three, 4-of-5 FT) and 6 rebounds in 23 minutes, his longest stint of the preseason. Jokić had 22 points (8-of-12), 11 rebounds and 10 assists in 26 minutes; the Denver Gazette notes the triple-double came before the third quarter ended. Johnson scored 15 (4-of-5 from three) and Murray 13 with 5 assists. Braun scored 2 but was a game-high +13. Off the bench, Julian Strawther scored 13, DaRon Holmes II 11 and Bryce Hopkins 10. The reserves finished the fourth quarter, with Ryan Nembhard, Cam Whitmore and Holmes scoring in the last five minutes. DeMar DeRozan went 0-of-4 (2 points, −10). For Chicago, Leonard Miller scored 26 off the bench (11-of-14), rookie Caleb Wilson 17 and Buddy Hield 15.

Marvin Bagley III did not play (box score: coach’s decision). The Denver Gazette says he was not available and Zeke Nnaji backed up Jokić. Mile High Sports says Bagley had minor discomfort and it was mostly a rest day. Spencer Jones (hamstring) also sat again. Adelman praised Gordon’s third quarter: “I’m not a doctor, but it seems like he feels good” (Denver Gazette). He also criticized the first half: “Our transition defense was embarrassing” (via FadeAway World). Chicago played without Nic Claxton, Zach Collins and rookie Tobe Awaka (Chicago Sun-Times). DraftKings closed Denver −7.5, so Chicago covered, and the 261 points went over the 235.5 total.
