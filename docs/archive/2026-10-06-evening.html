Last game · Preseason · Sun Oct 4 · Jazz 109, Nuggets 97

Utah won the preseason opener 109–97 at CU Events Center in Boulder before 7,860. The Jazz led 36–26 after one quarter and 62–52 at halftime, led for 88% of the game per ESPN, and built an 18-point lead; Denver’s biggest lead was 6. Utah outshot Denver 42% to 40% from the field and 15-of-45 to 11-of-39 from three, and outrebounded the Nuggets 54–47.

With Jamal Murray out (personal), Denver started Tyus Jones, Christian Braun, Cam Johnson, Aaron Gordon and Nikola Jokić. Julian Strawther led Denver with 14 points off the bench; Gordon added 12 in 10 minutes. Jokić took one shot (made it) and finished with 3 points, 7 assists, 3 rebounds, a steal, a block and no turnovers in 15 minutes. Jones had 8 points and 6 assists. With Spencer Jones out, rookie Trevon Brazile played backup power forward: 7 points (3-of-4), 3 rebounds, 2 blocks in 20 minutes. Lauri Markkanen led Utah with 17 points in 14 minutes; Keyonte George had 15, Blake Hinson 14 and Jaren Jackson Jr. 11.

Adelman on Jokić passing up shots: “I think there were six or seven times he could’ve just laid the ball in. If you want to create an atmosphere where people pass the ball to the open man, you have to do it in these moments in the preseason” (Mile High Sports). On Brazile facing Markkanen and Jackson: “Why wait? Welcome to the NBA, man” (Denver Gazette).

Today: No Nuggets roster transaction. RealGM’s league log for Oct 6 lists moves by Utah (waived Terrell Brown Jr.), Oklahoma City, the Clippers and Sacramento, none involving Denver. The latest Nuggets move is still Sep 30 (Ryan Nembhard signed to a two-way, KJ Simpson waived). ESPN and nba.com both list 21 players. Tonight’s rematch at Utah tips at 7:00 PM MDT; the Morning Edition will carry the result.
