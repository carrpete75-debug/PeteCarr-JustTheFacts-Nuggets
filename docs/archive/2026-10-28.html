Last game · Mon Oct 26 · Warriors 134, Nuggets 114

Denver led 32–28 after the first quarter. Golden State went on a 13–4 run in the second while Jokić rested, with two Georges Niang threes, and led 60–56 at halftime (Denver Post). Curry, scoreless for most of the first half, scored on the first possession of the third and had 17 points on 6-of-6 over the next eight minutes. The Warriors led 80–65 with 6:44 left in the third and won the quarter 40–28. A Brazile alley-oop cut it to 89–82, but the lead was back to 16 entering the fourth (Denver Post). It reached 24 (ESPN). Denver never led after halftime.

Gordon ejection: Down 107–91, Christian Braun was called for an offensive foul on a screen against Curry, his fifth foul. Gordon drew two technicals arguing the call and was ejected with 8:34 left (DenverSports; Denver Post; ClutchPoints). He finished with 7 points on 2-of-10. Adelman: “I thought Aaron was frustrated with the call. It just seemed like it was an inconsistent whistle in that moment” (DenverSports). Jokić came out with 3:33 left and Denver down 21 (AP). Many fans left early; after Saturday’s boos, there were none this time (DenverSports; Denver Post).

Denver: DeRozan 21 off the bench (7-of-15, 5-of-5 FT). Jokić 19 points (8-of-18, 1-of-6 from three), 7 rebounds, 11 assists, 3 blocks and a steal, so not a triple-double. Murray 19 points, 8 assists, 6 rebounds and 3 steals. Two-way rookie Bryce Hopkins made his second straight start and scored 13 (5-of-9), which Nuggets.com called the best game of his young career. Denver made 10 of 29 threes; Golden State made 22 of 44. The Warriors had 13 offensive rebounds and 23 second-chance points (Gazette). Denver forced 17 turnovers and had 13 steals. Adelman: “You have to put your hands on people if you want to have a chance. We didn’t do that” (Gazette; DenverSports). And: “We’re 1–2. There’s 79 games to go” (Gazette).

Jokić on booing, after the game: “I’m sticking to what I said … I’m not used to the fans booing their own team,” and fans who boo their own team “are not real fans” (Denver Post). Per the Post, after three games Denver ranks 26th in defensive rating and 25th in net rating, and the team is shooting 31.1% from three.

Warriors: Lendeborg 31 points, 12 rebounds, 6 assists (9-of-19, 12-of-13 FT). Curry 24 (8-of-8, 3-of-3, 5-of-5 FT), 21 of them after halftime. Podziemski 18 points and 7 rebounds, plus-33. Santos 13, Horford 11 (3-of-3 from three). Butler III, Moody and Porziņģis were out. Attendance: 19,520.

Betting result: DraftKings’ final ESPN listing was DEN −6.5 (−118), total 236.5, ML DEN −270 / GSW +220. Golden State won outright by 20, so it covered, and the 248 total went 11.5 over. Kalshi’s game market settled GSW Yes.

Report only, not a move: The Stein Line (via Hoops Rumors, Oct 26) says Denver and Sacramento could still be landing spots for Kevin Porter Jr., but notes Denver is far above the tax line.
