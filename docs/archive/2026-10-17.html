Last game · Preseason finale · Fri Oct 16 · Nuggets 121, Lakers 106

Denver closed its preseason with a 121–106 win at Crypto.com Arena, finishing 3–2. The Nuggets scored 36 in the first quarter for a 36–22 lead and led by as many as 17. The Lakers outscored Denver 33–26 in the second, so it was 62–55 at halftime. Denver led 88–83 after three, then won the fourth 33–23. Per ESPN, the Nuggets led for 96% of the game. Denver shot 46-of-79 (58%), 11-of-27 (41%) from three and 18-of-25 at the line. Los Angeles went 35-of-82 (43%), 17-of-43 (40%) and 19-of-25. Denver had a 56–30 edge in paint points and 12 steals to the Lakers’ 5, plus 32 assists to 26 and 45 rebounds to 39. Both teams had 14 turnovers. Attendance was 19,057.

With Aaron Gordon held out for rest, Trevon Brazile started alongside Murray, Braun, Cam Johnson and Jokić (Law Murray / The Athletic via RotoWire). Brazile had 12 points on 5-of-7, 8 rebounds, 2 steals and 2 blocks in 27 minutes. Jamal Murray led all scorers with 21 (8-of-15, 5-of-5 FT) and 6 assists. Nikola Jokić had 17 points on 7-of-8 (3-of-4 from three), 6 rebounds, 8 assists and 6 turnovers in 31 minutes. Christian Braun scored 16 and finished +18. Cam Johnson had 12 on 5-of-8 before he was ejected in the third quarter: a review upgraded his elbow to Austin Reaves’ face to a Flagrant 2 (USA Today; Lakers Nation). NBC Sports/Rotoworld notes the foul carries no regular-season punishment. Off the bench, DeMar DeRozan scored 14 (6-of-9) with 5 assists in 27 minutes, Marvin Bagley III had 13 points and 8 rebounds, and Tyus Jones had 8 assists without scoring. The Denver Gazette reports a nine-man rotation through three quarters.

For the Lakers, Austin Reaves scored 19 (8-of-12 FT). Luka Dončić had 15 in 19 first-half minutes, and Ziaire Williams had 14 on 5-of-6 in 11 minutes; both sat after halftime (Lakers Nation). Sandro Mamukelashvili had 15 and 8 rebounds, Jaden Hardy 13 and Quentin Grimes 11. DraftKings’ final ESPN pickcenter listing was LAL −3.5, total 231.5, DEN +124 (the evening card had LAL −5.5 / 229.5 / DEN +185). Denver won outright by 15, and the 227 points went under. Kalshi’s Oct 16 DEN market settled Yes.

Strawther diagnosis: Before the finale, the Nuggets announced that Julian Strawther has a left knee sprain from Wednesday’s game at the Clippers and will be re-evaluated in six weeks (AP/ESPN; Denver Gazette; Hoops Rumors). Adelman, before the game: “We were gonna rely on Jules a lot, especially on the offensive end … It’s unfortunate … especially with the timing for him” (DenverSports.com). His rookie-scale extension deadline is Mon Oct 19, and no extension has been reported. Without one, he is eligible for restricted free agency in 2027 (Hoops Rumors).

Transactions: No new Denver move overnight. NBA.com’s player-movement log (entries through Oct 16) and Spotrac’s Nuggets transactions list both show the Oct 14 Steven Crowl sign-and-waive as the latest Nuggets item. ESPN’s roster API still lists Crowl and waived Lonnie Walker IV (22 names; ESPN’s box score even listed Walker as DNP). nba.com lists 20, matching the contracts file. The roster count stays 20 (14 standard, 3 two-way, 3 Exhibit 10). Final cuts to 15 + 3 two-way are still to come before the Oct 22 opener.
