Two-way & Exhibit 10 rules

  • Two-way contracts pay a fixed $678,882 in 2026–27 and never count against the cap, tax or aprons. They become 50% guaranteed on the first day of the regular season (Oct 20). Two-way players can be active for up to 50 regular-season games and can't play in the playoffs unless promoted to a standard deal. Denver's three slots are full.
  • Exhibit 10 deals are one-year, non-guaranteed minimum contracts for camp. Waived by Oct 17, they cost nothing against the cap. The last day to convert one to a two-way deal is Oct 19, but Denver has no open two-way slot. A player kept into the season would take the 15th standard spot and add his minimum salary to the cap, tax and apron totals.

Sources: The CBA Guide (two-way salary, Exhibit 10 bonus) Hoops Rumors key dates Spotrac (guarantee dates)
